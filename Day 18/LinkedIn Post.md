📈 Problem: Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock
Today’s problem was about finding the maximum profit from buying a stock once and selling it later.
The key idea was simple:
🔹 Keep track of the minimum price seen so far
🔹 Calculate the profit if we sell at the current price
🔹 Keep updating the maximum profit
The important part was realizing that we don't need to compare every possible buy/sell combination. A single pass through the array is enough.
💡 Approach:
Minimum Price → Current Price → Maximum Profit
⏱️ Time Complexity: O(n)
💾 Space Complexity: O(1)
This was a good reminder that sometimes the best optimization isn't complicated—it comes from identifying exactly what information needs to be remembered while traversing the data.
