🚀 Day 9 of My Daily Problem Solving Challenge
Today's problem was Longest Consecutive Sequence.
The challenge wasn't just finding the longest sequence—it was doing it in O(n) time without sorting the array.
I solved it using a HashSet:
Store all numbers in a set.
Start counting only if the previous number doesn't exist.
Expand the sequence forward until it ends.
Keep track of the longest sequence found.
This approach avoids unnecessary work and achieves the required O(n) time complexity.
Every challenge like this reminds me that choosing the right data structure can completely change the efficiency of a solution.
